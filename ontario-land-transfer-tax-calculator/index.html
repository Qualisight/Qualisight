How Ontario land transfer tax works

Ontario charges land transfer tax when you buy a home. Each slice of the price is taxed at its own rate, like income tax. Only the part of the price inside a band pays that band's rate.

Part of the priceRate
Up to $55,0000.5%
$55,000 to $250,0001.0%
$250,000 to $400,0001.5%
$400,000 to $2,000,0002.0%
Over $2,000,000 (one or two single-family homes)2.5%

These rates have applied since January 1, 2017. Source: Calculating land transfer tax, Ontario Ministry of Finance.

A worked example

On a $750,000 home outside Toronto: $275 on the first $55,000, $1,950 on the next $195,000, $2,250 on the next $150,000 and $7,000 on the last $350,000. That is $11,475. A first-time buyer gets $4,000 back, so they pay $7,475. Those are the numbers the calculator starts with.

Toronto charges its own tax too

Inside the City of Toronto you pay Ontario's tax and Toronto's municipal land transfer tax. Toronto uses the same rates up to $3 million. Since April 1, 2026, homes with one or two single-family residences pay more above that:

$3,000,000 to $4,000,0004.40%
$4,000,000 to $5,000,0005.45%
$5,000,000 to $10,000,0006.50%
$10,000,000 to $20,000,0007.55%
Over $20,000,0008.60%

Source: Municipal land transfer tax rates, City of Toronto. Other Ontario cities do not charge their own land transfer tax.

First-time buyer refunds

Ontario refunds up to $4,000 of its tax. Toronto rebates up to $4,475 of its tax. A first-time buyer in Toronto can get up to $8,475 in total. To qualify for either one, you must:

  • be at least 18,
  • live in the home as your main home within nine months,
  • never have owned a home, or part of one, anywhere in the world. A spouse cannot have owned one while being your spouse, and
  • be a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, or become one within 18 months.

Most lawyers claim the refund for you at closing. If not, you can apply within 18 months. When only some of the buyers are first-time buyers, the refund is cut to their share. Sources: Ontario refunds for first-time homebuyers and Toronto rebates.

Common questions

How much is land transfer tax in Ontario?

It depends on the price. Ontario charges 0.5% on the first $55,000, 1% up to $250,000, 1.5% up to $400,000, 2% up to $2 million, and 2.5% above $2 million for a home with one or two single-family residences. On a $500,000 home that is $6,475.

How much is the first-time buyer refund in Ontario?

Up to $4,000 off the provincial land transfer tax. That covers all of the tax on a home up to about $368,000. Buyers in the City of Toronto can also get up to $4,475 off the Toronto tax, for up to $8,475 in total.

Do I pay land transfer tax twice in Toronto?

Yes. Inside the City of Toronto you pay Ontario's land transfer tax and Toronto's municipal land transfer tax. Toronto's rates match Ontario's up to $3 million, then rise in steps from 4.40% to 8.60% for homes with one or two single-family residences since April 1, 2026.

Who counts as a first-time buyer for the refund?

You must be at least 18, live in the home as your main home within nine months, and never have owned a home anywhere in the world. A spouse cannot have owned a home while being your spouse. You also need to be a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, or become one within 18 months.

When do I pay land transfer tax?

At closing, in cash, usually through your real estate lawyer. It cannot be added to your mortgage, so save for it on top of your down payment.

What this calculator assumes

The home has one or two single-family residences (a house, semi, townhouse or condo unit), every buyer is a Canadian resident, and the price is the full amount paid. It does not include Ontario's Non-Resident Speculation Tax, which applies to some foreign buyers. If only some buyers are first-time buyers, your refund will be smaller than shown.
