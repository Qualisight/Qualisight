What protects your data

  • Stored in Canada. Your account and the numbers you enter live in Supabase's Canada Central region, in Montreal.
  • Encrypted. Supabase encrypts stored data with AES-256 and all connections with TLS. Supabase's security page.
  • Locked to your account. The database only lets a signed-in account read and change its own row. Another user cannot open yours.
  • Sign-in rules. Passwords need at least 8 characters, and new accounts confirm their email address.
  • Nothing sold. Your information is never sold, rented or shared for advertising. The site's page counts are anonymous and never include what you type.
  • You can leave any time. Delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, or email privacy@qualisight.ca.

Where some data leaves Canada

Three services process some information in the United States, only for what they do:

  • Stripe handles payments if you subscribe. Your card goes to Stripe, not to Qualisight.
  • Anthropic writes the AI coach's answers. When you ask the coach a question, your question and the figures needed to answer it are sent to Anthropic. If you never use the coach, nothing goes there. See how the AI coach works.
  • Zoho Mail sends and receives email.

What is not in place yet

To be straight with you:

  • No independent security audit yet. Qualisight relies on Supabase, which says it is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant, but the app itself has not been audited by an outside firm.
  • A one-person business. There is no security team. Problems are fixed by Lucas, and you can report one to hello@qualisight.ca. The same contact is listed in security.txt.
  • Not a bank or an advisor. Qualisight is a budgeting and education tool. It is not a financial institution, and it is not registered to give investment advice.

Good habits with any money app

The Financial Consumer Agency of Canada suggests reading an app's privacy and security policies before sharing financial information. Read the FCAC's guidance. With Qualisight you can also try the sample account first, with no sign-up, and use nicknames instead of real account numbers.
