Where the rules come from

Every rule is taken from the body that sets it, and each tool page links to its source and shows the date it was last checked: the Canada Revenue Agency for TFSA, RRSP and FHSA limits, CMHC and the Government of Canada for down payments and mortgage insurance, OSFI for the stress test, the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada for credit card math, and the Ontario Ministry of Finance and the City of Toronto for land transfer tax. Nothing is written from memory.

When we check again

Every January, when new yearly limits apply. Every December, when OSFI reviews the stress test rate. And any time a federal or Ontario budget changes a rule. When a rule changes, the calculator, its worked example and this page are updated together.

What the calculators are not

They are estimates for planning. Your lender, your card issuer and the CRA have the final word: a bank can be stricter than the minimum rules, and your CRA account shows your exact TFSA room. Qualisight gives information, not financial advice, and never recommends specific investments.

About the AI coach

The AI coach in the app answers general questions about Canadian money rules using the numbers you entered. It can be wrong. For anything that affects your taxes or a big decision, check the answer with the CRA or a licensed professional. It is not a licensed financial advisor.

Your data

The free calculators collect nothing you type into them. The app needs no bank login: you type or paste your numbers, they are encrypted, never sold, and you can delete everything any time. Read the full privacy policy and terms.
