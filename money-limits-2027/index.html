RRSP: $35,390 for 2027

Your new RRSP room for 2027 is 18% of what you earned in 2026, up to $35,390, minus any pension adjustment from a work pension plan. Unused room from past years carries forward. The CRA has also set 2028 at $36,390. Work out your 2027 RRSP room.

Source: CRA limits table.

TFSA: not announced yet

As of November 22, 2026, the CRA has not published the 2027 TFSA limit. It was $7,000 in 2024, 2025 and 2026, and total room since 2009 reached $109,000 for someone who was 18 in 2009. The limit follows inflation in $500 steps, so many writers expect $7,500, but nothing is official yet. See your room on January 1 under both cases.

FHSA: $8,000 a year, $40,000 for life

You get $8,000 of room each year from the year you open your first FHSA, and up to $8,000 of unused room carries into the next year. Opening an account before December 31, 2026 means up to $16,000 of room in 2027. Check your FHSA room.

Source: CRA, First Home Savings Account.

Buying a home

The minimum down payment is 5% of the first $500,000, plus 10% of the part from $500,000 to $1.5 million. Homes at $1.5 million or more need 20%. In Ontario, first-time buyers can get up to $4,000 back on land transfer tax, plus up to $4,475 more inside the City of Toronto. Down payment calculator and land transfer tax calculator.

Sources: FCAC, down payment, Ontario refunds and Toronto rebates.

Common questions

What is the RRSP limit for 2027?

$35,390. Your own new room is 18% of your 2026 earned income, up to $35,390, minus any pension adjustment. The deadline to contribute for the 2026 tax year is March 1, 2027.

What is the TFSA limit for 2027?

The Canada Revenue Agency had not announced it as of November 22, 2026. It was $7,000 for 2024, 2025 and 2026. Many writers expect $7,500 because of how the limit follows inflation, but it is not official until the CRA publishes it.

What is the FHSA limit for 2027?

$8,000 of new room, plus up to $8,000 carried forward from 2026 if you opened an account and did not use it, so up to $16,000. The lifetime limit is $40,000.

When is the 2027 tax deadline?

April 30, 2027 for most people to file their 2026 return and pay any balance owing. If you or your spouse are self-employed, you can file by June 15, 2027, but any balance owing is still due April 30.
