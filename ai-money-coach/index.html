What it will not do

  • Pick stocks, ETFs or crypto, or tell you to buy or sell anything.
  • Guess a limit or rate that is not on its checked list. It says to check the CRA instead.
  • Tell you your own TFSA, RRSP or FHSA room. Only your CRA My Account has that.
  • Replace a licensed advisor. It is an education tool, and it can be wrong.
  • Recommend loans, lenders or debt settlement companies. If you are struggling, it points you to free non-profit credit counselling.

Common questions

Is the Qualisight AI coach free?

You can try it with the sample account for free, with no sign-up. With your own account, the coach is part of the 14-day free trial, then part of the $12 CAD a month plan. Budgeting and debt payoff stay free either way.

Is it financial advice?

No. It explains your own numbers and how Canadian rules work, for education. It is not a licensed financial advisor and it can be wrong. Check anything about taxes or a big decision with the CRA or a licensed professional.

Will it tell me which stocks or ETFs to buy?

No. It never recommends buying, selling or holding a specific stock, ETF or crypto. It can describe what you already own and explain general ideas, like why paying off high-interest debt usually comes first.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank login. The coach only sees the numbers you typed into Qualisight yourself.

Which AI does it use?

Claude, made by Anthropic. Your question and the figures needed to answer it are sent to Anthropic to write the reply. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it know the 2027 TFSA limit?

It knows what has been published. As of November 25, 2026 the CRA has not announced the 2027 TFSA limit, so the coach says so instead of guessing. It uses a short list of figures checked against CRA, FCAC and Ontario sources, each with the date it was checked.
