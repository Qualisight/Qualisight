About Qualisight

Qualisight is a free Canadian budgeting and debt payoff app, plus a set of free money calculators. It is built and run by one person, Lucas, a business graduate in Ontario.

What it does

Qualisight turns your money into pictures you can watch change:

  • Debt: type in what you owe and see the month you will be debt-free. Your debt shows as a storm that clears as you pay.
  • Budget: split your pay into jars, and see what is left at the end of the month.
  • Home: see the month your down payment is ready.
  • Investing: track your TFSA, RRSP and FHSA as a growing grove of trees.

There is no bank login. You type in your own numbers, so nothing connects to your bank.

What is free

Budgeting, debt payoff and the dashboard are free forever. The investing grove, the home planner and the AI coach are free for 14 days, with no card needed. After that they cost CA$12 a month if you choose to subscribe. Every calculator on this site is free and needs no account.

How each calculator works

Every tool page says what it assumes and when its rules were last checked. Here is the short version, with the official source for each rule.

Debt payoff calculator

Runs your debts month by month. Interest is added at one twelfth of the yearly rate, every minimum is paid, and extra money goes to one debt at a time (smallest balance first for snowball, highest rate first for avalanche).

Background: Financial Consumer Agency of Canada, Paying back your debt
Credit card payoff calculator

Adds interest each month at one twelfth of the yearly rate, then takes off your payment. The minimum payment compares a percentage of the balance (3% by default) with a $10 floor, the same method as the Government of Canada's own example.

Check it against: FCAC credit card payment calculator
Budget calculator

Adds up your monthly income and spending and shows what is left. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Source: FCAC, Making a budget
Emergency fund calculator

Sets a target of 3 to 6 months of essential costs and works out the month you reach it at your saving pace.

Source: FCAC, Setting up an emergency fund
TFSA room calculator and TFSA limit 2027

Adds up the yearly TFSA limits from the year you turned 18 (or 2009, whichever is later), less what you put in, plus what you took out in past years.

Source: Canada Revenue Agency, limits table
RRSP limit 2027

18% of last year's earned income, up to the yearly dollar limit, plus unused room carried forward.

Source: Canada Revenue Agency, limits table
FHSA limit 2027

$8,000 a year, up to $40,000 for life, with up to $8,000 of unused room carried into the next year.

Source: Canada Revenue Agency, FHSA
Down payment calculator

5% of the first $500,000, 10% of the part from $500,000 to $1.5 million, and 20% at $1.5 million or more. Adds the mortgage insurance premium when you put down less than 20%.

Sources: FCAC, How much you need for a down payment and CMHC, Mortgage loan insurance explained
mortgage affordability calculator, Ontario land transfer tax calculator

Taxes each slice of the price at Ontario's rate, adds Toronto's tax for homes in the city, then takes off first-time buyer refunds of up to $4,000 (Ontario) and $4,475 (Toronto).

Sources: Ontario Ministry of Finance and City of Toronto
TSX 60 heatmap, S&P 500 heatmap and bank earnings dates

Prices come from public market data and can be delayed. Earnings dates come from each bank's own investor relations page.

What Qualisight will not do

  • Ask for your bank login.
  • Send what you type into a calculator anywhere. The calculators only count page visits, with no numbers attached.
  • Sell, rent or share your data for advertising.
  • Tell you which stock or fund to buy. Everything here is information to help you understand your money, not financial advice.

Keeping the numbers right

Canadian limits and rules change every year. Each rule is checked against the CRA, the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada or CMHC, and every page shows the date it was last checked. All pages are checked again each January and whenever a federal budget changes a rule.

Found a number that looks wrong? Email hello@qualisight.ca. It gets fixed and the page's date is updated.
