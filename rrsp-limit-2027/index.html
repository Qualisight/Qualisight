How your RRSP room is worked out

Each year the CRA adds new room based on what you earned the year before. The new room is 18% of your earned income from the previous year, up to that year's dollar limit. If you are in a pension plan at work, your pension adjustment is taken off. Any room you did not use in past years carries forward and is added on top. The CRA explains it here.

So for 2027, the new room is 18% of your 2026 earned income, up to $35,390, minus your 2026 pension adjustment.

The deadline for 2026 is March 1, 2027

You have until March 1, 2027 to put money in and deduct it on your 2026 tax return. Contributions from January 1 to March 1, 2027 can count for either 2026 or 2027. After March 1, they count for 2027 only. December 31 of the year you turn 71 is the last day you can put money into your own RRSP.

If you put in more than your limit, the first $2,000 over is allowed. Anything past that is taxed 1% a month until it is taken out. CRA rules on excess contributions.

Common questions

What is the RRSP limit for 2027?

The Canada Revenue Agency has set the 2027 RRSP dollar limit at $35,390, up from $33,810 in 2026. That is the most new room anyone can get for 2027. Your own new room is 18% of your 2026 earned income, up to $35,390, minus any pension adjustment from a work pension plan.

What is the RRSP deadline for the 2026 tax year?

March 1, 2027. Money you put in by that day can be deducted on your 2026 tax return. Contributions made from January 1 to March 1, 2027 can be claimed on either your 2026 or your 2027 return.

How much do I need to earn to get the full $35,390?

About $196,612 of earned income in 2026, because 18% of that is $35,390. Earn less and your new room is 18% of what you earned. Earn more and it stops at $35,390.

Where do I find my exact RRSP room?

Your notice of assessment shows your RRSP deduction limit for the next year. You can also see it in CRA My Account. The 2027 figure will appear on the notice for your 2026 return, after you file in spring 2027.

What happens if I put in too much?

You can go up to $2,000 over your limit without a penalty, if you were 18 or older the year before. Past that, the CRA charges a tax of 1% per month on the extra amount until it is taken out or new room covers it.

What is the RRSP limit for 2028?

The CRA has already set it at $36,390. It applies to room created from your 2027 earned income.
