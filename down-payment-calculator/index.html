How the minimum down payment works

In Canada, the least you can put down depends on the price of the home:

Home priceMinimum down payment
$500,000 or less5% of the price
$500,000 to under $1.5 million5% of the first $500,000, plus 10% of the rest
$1.5 million or more20% of the price

The $1.5 million line moved up from $1 million in December 2024. Below 20% down, the mortgage must be insured, and the insurance is not available at $1.5 million or more.

A worked example

A $650,000 home needs $25,000 (5% of the first $500,000) plus $15,000 (10% of the other $150,000). That is $40,000, or about 6.2% of the price. With $12,000 saved and $900 a month going in, the gap of $28,000 takes 32 months. Those are the numbers the calculator starts with. Swap in your own.

What mortgage insurance costs

With less than 20% down, you pay a one-time premium. It is a percentage of the mortgage, not the price, and it is usually added to the mortgage so you pay it off over time.

Down paymentPremium on the mortgage
5% to under 10%4.00%
10% to under 15%3.10%
15% to under 20%2.80%
20% or moreNone needed

In Ontario, Quebec and Saskatchewan, provincial sales tax on the premium is due in cash at closing. It cannot be added to the mortgage. A 30-year amortization can add a small surcharge.

Do not forget closing costs

Land transfer tax, a lawyer, an inspection and moving often add 1.5% to 4% of the price. These are paid in cash and are not part of the down payment, so save for both. First-time buyers can get land transfer tax rebates in some provinces and cities.

Common questions

What is the minimum down payment in Canada?

5% of the first $500,000, plus 10% of any part above $500,000, for homes under $1.5 million. At $1.5 million or more it is 20%.

How much is the minimum on a $600,000 home?

$35,000. That is $25,000 on the first $500,000 plus $10,000 on the other $100,000.

Is it worth waiting until I have 20%?

Waiting saves the insurance premium, but home prices and rents can change while you wait. Neither choice is always right. The calculator shows both dates so you can see the trade.

How does an FHSA help?

A First Home Savings Account lets you put in up to $8,000 a year, up to $40,000 in total. What you put in can lower your income tax, and money taken out for a qualifying first home is not taxed. Up to $8,000 of unused room carries forward to the next year once the account is open.

Can the down payment be a gift?

Often yes, from a close family member, with a signed gift letter. Lenders have their own rules, so ask yours early.

What this calculator assumes

It uses the federal minimum down payment rules and CMHC's standard premium rates for an owner-occupied home with a 25-year amortization. It assumes your savings earn nothing and that the price stays the same. Lenders can ask for more than the minimum, and you also need to pass the stress test.
