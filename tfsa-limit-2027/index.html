How the TFSA limit is set

The TFSA started in 2009 with a $5,000 yearly limit. Each year that amount is adjusted for inflation and rounded to the nearest $500. Because of the rounding, the limit stays flat for a few years and then moves up by $500. It has been $7,000 since 2024. If inflation since then has pushed the adjusted amount past the halfway point to the next step, the 2027 limit becomes $7,500; if not, it stays at $7,000.

Unused room never expires. Every year's limit adds to your total from the year you turn 18, whether or not you have opened a TFSA.

Common questions

What is the TFSA limit for 2027?

The Canada Revenue Agency has not announced it yet. Its limits table, last updated October 28, 2026, lists $7,000 for 2026 and no figure for 2027. Many Canadian finance writers expect $7,500 because of how the limit follows inflation, but treat that as an estimate until the CRA confirms it.

When will the 2027 TFSA limit be announced?

The CRA usually confirms the next year's limit in late November or early December. This page will be updated the day it is published.

How is the TFSA limit set?

The limit started at $5,000 in 2009 and is adjusted for inflation each year, rounded to the nearest $500. That is why it stays the same for a few years and then jumps by $500.

How much total TFSA room will I have on January 1, 2027?

If you were 18 or older and living in Canada in 2009 and never contributed, your room through 2026 is $109,000. Add the 2027 limit on top: $116,500 if it is $7,500, or $116,000 if it stays at $7,000. Use the calculator above for your own numbers.

Do TFSA withdrawals give the room back?

Yes, but not right away. Money you take out is added back to your room on January 1 of the next year. So a withdrawal made in 2026 becomes new room on January 1, 2027, on top of the 2027 limit.

What happens if I put in too much?

The CRA charges a tax of 1% per month on the highest amount you were over the limit in that month, until you take it out or new room opens. Check your exact room in CRA My Account before a big contribution.
