How FHSA room works

You get $8,000 of room in the year you open your first FHSA. Each year after that you get another $8,000. Room you do not use carries into the next year, but only up to $8,000. The total you can ever put in is $40,000. The CRA explains it here.

Room only starts once you open an account. Years before you opened one give you nothing, and the carryforward is $0 in the year you open it.

Why December 31 matters

Say you qualify and open an FHSA in December 2026 with nothing in it. Your 2026 room of $8,000 goes unused, so it carries forward, and in 2027 you can put in up to $16,000. Wait until January 2027 to open it and your 2027 room is $8,000. Your $40,000 lifetime limit is the same either way; opening sooner lets you reach it sooner.

Money has to go in by December 31 to count against that year's room. Unlike an RRSP, there is no 60-day window in the new year.

Common questions

What is the FHSA limit for 2027?

$8,000 of new room for 2027, plus up to $8,000 of unused room carried forward from 2026, so at most $16,000. The lifetime limit stays at $40,000. As of November 17, 2026 no change to these amounts has been announced.

Does unused FHSA room carry forward?

Yes, but only up to $8,000. Room you do not use in a year rolls into the next year, capped at $8,000. Anything above that does not roll forward, though it does not come off your $40,000 lifetime limit.

How many FHSAs can I have?

As many as you like, but they share one limit. Your $8,000 of yearly room and the $40,000 lifetime limit count across all of your FHSAs together. A second account does not give you more room. Source: Canada Revenue Agency.

Should I open an FHSA before the end of 2026?

That is your call, and you must qualify first. The rule to know: room only starts the year you open your first FHSA. Open one in 2026, even with $0 in it, and you can carry $8,000 into 2027 for $16,000 of room. Open it in 2027 and you start with $8,000.

Who can open an FHSA?

You must be 18 or older, a resident of Canada, and a first-time home buyer. For opening an FHSA, that means you did not live in a home that you or your spouse or common-law partner owned, in the current year before opening or in the four calendar years before.

When does my FHSA have to be used?

The account can stay open until December 31 of the year of the earliest of these: the 15th anniversary of opening your first FHSA, the year you turn 71, or the year after your first qualifying withdrawal for a home.

What if I put in too much?

Going over your room creates an excess FHSA amount. The CRA generally charges a tax of 1% per month on the excess while it stays in the account.
