How to read a bank's results

Earnings per share (EPS) is the profit for the quarter split across every share. Banks report two versions: reported, which counts everything, and adjusted, which leaves out one-time items like restructuring costs. Headlines usually compare the adjusted number with what analysts expected.

Provisions for credit losses are money a bank sets aside for loans it expects will not be paid back. When this number rises, the bank expects more borrowers to struggle.

The fourth quarter is also when banks release their full-year results, and they often announce dividend changes at the same time.

Common questions

When do the Big Six Canadian banks report fourth quarter 2026 earnings?

Scotiabank and BMO report on Wednesday, December 2, 2026. RBC, TD and CIBC report on Thursday, December 3. National Bank reports on Friday, December 4. Each date is taken from the bank's own announcement.

Why do Canadian banks report in early December?

The big Canadian banks end their fiscal year on October 31, so their fourth quarter covers August to October and results come out about five weeks later. That is also when their full-year results are released.

What time do the banks release results?

Before the stock market opens, usually between 5:30 and 7:30 a.m. Eastern, followed by a conference call with analysts. The time for each bank is listed on its card above.

What currency are the analyst estimates in?

The estimates on this page come from a US earnings calendar that tracks the banks' New York listings, so they are in US dollars. The banks report in Canadian dollars. Compare an estimate with the result from the same calendar, not with the Canadian figures in the bank's news release.
