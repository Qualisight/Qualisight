How to download your bank transactions as a CSV file

Most Canadian banks let you save your recent transactions as a spreadsheet file, called a CSV, from their website. Here are the steps for each bank, then how to turn that file into a budget without giving anyone your bank login.

Steps checked November 26, 2026 against each bank's own help page where one exists. Banks change their websites, so the button names may differ a little.

Before you start

  • Use a computer. Most banks only offer downloads on their website, not in the app.
  • Pick one month, or this month so far.
  • When asked for a format, choose CSV, "Spreadsheet" or "Excel CSV".

Steps for your bank

RBC Royal Bank

  1. Sign in to RBC Online Banking on a computer. User guides say the mobile app does not offer CSV downloads.
  2. Open the account you want.
  3. Select Download above the list of transactions.
  4. Choose CSV and a date range, then download.

User guides say the CSV covers roughly the last 90 to 180 days. We could not find an RBC Canada help page with these steps, so they come from published user guides.

No official help page found. Checked November 26, 2026.

TD Canada Trust

  1. Sign in to EasyWeb on a computer and open the Accounts page.
  2. Select the account. On the Account Activity page, pick the time period or one month.
  3. At the bottom, choose Spreadsheet (.CSV) next to Download, then select Download. The file is usually called accountactivity.csv.

TD lists downloading account activity as an EasyWeb feature. The detailed steps come from user guides. If a credit card does not offer a CSV, use the paste option below.

Source: TD: EasyWeb online banking. Checked November 26, 2026.

Scotiabank

  1. Sign in to Scotiabank online banking on a computer. Scotiabank says the app cannot download transaction history yet.
  2. Select your account.
  3. Choose to download your transactions, pick CSV and the dates, then download.

Source: Scotiabank Help Centre. Checked November 26, 2026.

BMO

  1. Sign in to BMO Online Banking and open the account.
  2. Select Download above the transactions and pick a date range.
  3. Choose the CSV (spreadsheet) format, then download.

BMO says you can download bank accounts and BMO Mastercard accounts, but not loans, mortgages or lines of credit. There is no charge.

Source: BMO Online Banking Help Centre. Checked November 26, 2026.

CIBC

  1. Sign in to CIBC Online Banking on a computer.
  2. From the home page, under More, select Download Transactions.
  3. Pick the account, the dates and the CSV format, then download.

Source: CIBC: how to check balances and download transactions. Checked November 26, 2026.

Tangerine

  1. Sign in at tangerine.ca on a computer. Tangerine says this does not work in the mobile app.
  2. Go to Transactions.
  3. Select Download Transactions, choose the Excel CSV format, and follow the prompts.

Source: Tangerine: How do I download transactions?. Checked November 26, 2026.

Simplii Financial

  1. Sign in to Simplii online banking on a computer.
  2. Look for Download Transactions, pick the account, dates and CSV, then download.

Simplii is a division of CIBC. We could not find a Simplii help page with these steps, so treat them as a starting point and ask Simplii if the option is missing.

No official help page found. Checked November 26, 2026.

Wealthsimple

  1. Sign in to Wealthsimple on the website and go to your documents.
  2. Choose Custom activity statement, pick the time period, then select Next.
  3. Pick your accounts and select Download CSV. Your chequing activity comes as its own file whose name starts with chequing-activities.

Source: Wealthsimple Help Centre. Checked November 26, 2026.

American Express Canada

  1. Sign in to your Amex Online Services account on a computer.
  2. Open your statements and activity, and look for a download option. Choose CSV if it is offered.

Amex Canada says you can download statements from the last six months. Card holders report that a website redesign removed the recent transactions download, so you may only see PDF statements. If that happens, use the paste option below instead.

Source: American Express Canada: Online Services. Checked November 26, 2026.

Desjardins

  1. Sign in to AccèsD in a web browser. Desjardins says this does not work in the mobile app.
  2. On the AccèsD home page, select the Search transactions tab.
  3. Select the link to download your transaction history, choose CSV, then download.

Source: Desjardins: How to download your transaction history. Checked November 26, 2026.

Common questions

Is it safe to download my transactions?

Yes, the file goes from your bank's website to your own computer. Treat it like a bank statement: keep it private and delete it when you are done.

Does Qualisight upload my bank file?

No. Qualisight reads the file in your browser, shows you every purchase, and saves only the ones you tick. The file itself is not uploaded.

Why not just connect my bank?

Qualisight never asks for your bank login, on purpose. A CSV file gives you the same transactions without handing over your password.

I can't find a CSV option. What now?

Use a computer, not the app, since most banks only offer downloads on their website. If there is still no CSV, copy the list of transactions from your online banking page and paste it into Qualisight instead.

How far back can I download?

It depends on the bank, often a few months. Qualisight's budget works one month at a time, so this month's transactions are all you need.
